About

Grounded in aviation.
Building in cybersecurity.

Jacqueline was born in Tanzania, East Africa. At eleven years old, her father began taking her to Young Eagles events, where young people fly with certified flight instructors to airports across the region — a program built to inspire the next generation of pilots. It worked. Her favorite airports became Brookhaven, where she took her first flight, and Republic Airport in Farmingdale, where she trained with the Brookhaven Aviation Flying Club.

She earned her Associate in Science in Aviation Management from Dutchess Community College in 2024, alongside an Aviation Science (Pilot) major that led to her FAA Student Pilot Certificate. That same problem-solving mindset carried her into a new direction: she's now pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science / Cybersecurity at the University at Albany, SUNY, expected 2027 — building interpreters, studying real-world breaches, and developing risk-based defense strategies alongside her coursework in data structures, networking, and operating systems.
