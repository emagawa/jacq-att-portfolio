About Jacqueline

Profile

Grounded in aviation · Disciplined in science · Building in cybersecurity.

I am an Aviation Management graduate (A.S., Dutchess Community College, 2024) now completing my B.S. in Computer Science with a minor in Cybersecurity at the University at Albany (expected 2027). My path runs from airport ramps to security operations — driven by the same discipline either way.

It started at eleven, flying Young Eagles events out of Brookhaven and Republic Airport, where I trained with the Brookhaven Aviation Flying Club and earned my FAA Student Pilot Certificate. That same checklist mindset now shapes how I approach code and security.

Today, I build interpreters from scratch, dissect real-world breaches like SolarWinds, and work fluently in Java, C, and Linux. I am looking for my first role where IT, cybersecurity, and aviation intersect, while I remain highly motivated to start as a Flight Attendant to launch my aviation career from the inside out.
